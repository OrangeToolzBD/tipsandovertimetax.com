Contact

Have a question, spotted a figure that looks out of date, or want to suggest an improvement to Tips & Overtime Tax? We would genuinely like to hear from you - email us and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Email us

The best way to reach us is by email at REPLACE_CONTACT_EMAIL. A real person reads every message. To help us reply quickly, it is useful if you include:

  • The page or tool you are asking about - a link is ideal.
  • What you expected to see, and what you saw instead.
  • For a figure you think is wrong, the source you are comparing it with.

We usually reply within a few business days. We cannot offer one-to-one tax advice, but we are always happy to explain how a tool works or fix something that is not right.

Reporting an error

Accuracy matters to us, especially for the federal deductions for tips and overtime pay. If a number looks wrong or a rule has changed, tell us which page it is on and what the correct figure should be, ideally with a link to IRS.gov. We check every report against the official source and update the page - and its "Updated" date - when it needs it.

What we can and cannot help with

We can answer questions about how the calculators and guides work, correct mistakes, and take suggestions for new tools or clearer explanations. What we cannot do is give you personalised tax advice or make decisions for your own situation - for that, please speak to a qualified tax professional or the IRS.
