Who it's for

Built for people who earn tips and overtime

If you wait tables, tend bar, style hair, drive deliveries, or clock overtime on the floor or the ward, the 2025 law created two federal deductions aimed squarely at you. They lower the income tax on your tips and on the premium half of your overtime, up to generous annual caps.

These calculators translate the rules into a real dollar figure for your situation — so you're not left guessing what the headlines mean for your paycheck.

Why trust these numbers

Straight from the IRS, dated and linked

Every figure comes from official IRS guidance — the newsroom explainer and the draft Schedule 1-A instructions — not from other calculator sites. The caps ($12,500 overtime, $25,000 tips), the phase-out thresholds ($150,000 single / $300,000 joint) and the SSN and joint-filing rules are drawn straight from those sources.

On each calculator we link the exact IRS source with the date we last checked it, and update the same week the IRS changes a number. These are estimates to help you plan — not tax advice. Confirm with IRS.gov or a tax professional before you file.
